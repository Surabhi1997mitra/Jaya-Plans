Insulin resistance protocol
3 changes that matter
starting now.
1 — Low-GI carbs only
Roti (wheat, bajra, jowar), dal (all types), rajma, chickpeas. No white rice at dinner. No bread at dinner. Carbs are front-loaded to breakfast and lunch when insulin sensitivity is higher.
This isn't about avoiding carbs — it's about choosing carbs your body handles better. Roti + dal is your foundation.
2 — Paneer once per day (choose one meal)
Pick one meal — breakfast, lunch, or dinner — to include paneer (80–100g). Rest of protein comes from dal, rajma, chickpeas, and Beast Life shake post-workout. This keeps the plan simpler and your budget sustainable.
3 — Daily steps: 5,000 minimum
You're currently at <2,000. Movement after meals is non-negotiable — even 10 minutes of slow walking after lunch and dinner directly reduces blood sugar spikes. Build to 5,000 steps over 2–3 weeks.
This is not about exercise — it's about managing insulin resistance. Walk after eating. Every day.
Water & hydration
Current: 1–2L per day → Target: 3L per day
Dehydration worsens insulin resistance and makes fat loss harder. By week 2, aim for 2.5–3L. Keep a bottle on your desk — most of this comes from sipping during work hours, not huge gulps.
Timeline & expectations
6-month fat loss phase
Month 1–2: Build habits. Paneer every meal, 3,000+ steps, 2.5L water. Weight may drop 2–3 kg as water weight leaves. Energy will improve noticeably by week 3.

Month 3–4: Consistent fat loss phase. Add 1–2 kg of muscle from home workouts. Insulin sensitivity improving. Continue building steps to 5,000–7,000.

Month 5–6: Refinement. If progress slows, we adjust macros slightly. By end of 6 months, new habits are automatic and you feel like a different version of yourself.

Important reminders
🦴 Bone & Joint Safety: Move slowly. Control the movement. No jerking. If anything causes sharp pain (not muscle fatigue), stop immediately. Breathe steadily through every rep.
✓ Before every session: 5–10 min gentle warmup. After: 5–10 min stretching and cool-down.
Lower Body 1 & 2 (Repeat Same)
Both lower days are identical.
Solid, proven exercises.
Warmup (5–10 min)
· Leg swings: 10 forward-back, 10 side-to-side (each leg)
· Hip circles: 10 each direction (each hip)
· Bodyweight squats: 10 reps (get movement going)
· March in place: 1 min
Main Workout
1. Bodyweight Squats — Free Squats
· Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
· Lower hips back and down (knees bent, chest up)
· Go as deep as comfortable (full depth preferred)
· Push through heels to stand back up
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

2. Reverse Lunges — Controlled Movement
· Stand with feet hip-width apart
· Step backward with right leg, lower hips until both knees bend 90°
· Front knee stays above ankle, back knee hovers above ground
· Push back to standing
· Alternate legs
· Repeat: 3 sets × 8–10 reps total (4–5 each leg)

3. Glute Bridges — Squeeze at the Top
· Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat on floor (hip-width apart)
· Push through heels to lift hips up (squeeze glutes at top)
· Hold for 2 seconds
· Lower slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

4. Bottle Holding RDLs (Romanian Deadlifts)
· Hold a bottle (water or sand) in one hand or both hands at chest/sides
· Stand with feet hip-width apart, slight knee bend
· Hinge at hips, lowering the bottle toward the ground
· Feel the stretch in hamstrings (don't round your back)
· Drive hips forward to stand back up
· Repeat: 3 sets × 10–12 reps

5. Calf Raises — Simple & Effective
· Stand with feet hip-width apart (hold wall for balance if needed)
· Rise up onto toes (ball of feet)
· Hold for 2 seconds at the top
· Lower heels back down slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 15–20 reps

6. Leg Raise & Crunches (Ab Exercise) Core work
· Lie on back, legs extended
· Raise legs up to 90° (keep knees slightly bent if needed)
· Lower legs slowly (don't touch floor, keep tension)
· That's 1 leg raise rep
· After 8–10 leg raises, do crunches: bend knees, crunch torso toward knees
· Repeat: 3 sets × 8–10 leg raises + 10–12 crunches
Cool-down (5–10 min)
· Quad stretch: 30 sec each leg
· Hip flexor stretch (low lunge): 30 sec each side
· Hamstring stretch: 30 sec
· Glute stretch (figure-4 on back): 30 sec each side
Push Day (Upper Body 1)
Chest, shoulders, arms.
Simple + effective.
Warmup (5–10 min)
· Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
· Shoulder rolls: 10 forward, 10 backward
· Cat-cow stretch: 10 reps
· Arm swings: 10 each direction
Main Workout
1. Wall Push-ups
· Stand arm's length away from wall, hands on wall at shoulder height
· Lean forward, bending elbows (chest moves toward wall)
· Push back to starting position
· Keep body straight
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

2. Lateral Raises with Dumbbells (Bottles Filled with Water/Sand)
· Stand with feet hip-width apart, dumbbells at sides
· Raise both arms out to sides to shoulder height
· Hold for 1 second
· Lower slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

3. Front Raises with Dumbbells
· Stand with feet hip-width apart, dumbbells at thighs
· Raise both arms forward to shoulder height
· Hold for 1 second
· Lower slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

4. Planks Core
· Hands on ground (or elbows) shoulder-width apart
· Body in straight line from head to heels
· Tighten core, don't let hips sag
· Hold: 3 sets × 20–40 seconds

5. Mountain Climbers Core
· Start in plank position
· Bring right knee toward chest, then back (quickly)
· Bring left knee toward chest, then back
· Continue alternating in a running motion
· Keep core tight, hips level
· Repeat: 3 sets × 20–30 seconds (or 10–15 reps each leg)
Cool-down (5–10 min)
· Chest stretch: 30 sec
· Shoulder stretch (arm across chest): 30 sec each arm
· Triceps stretch: 30 sec each arm
· Child's pose: 45 sec
Back Day (Upper Body 2)
Back, biceps, triceps, core.
Balanced upper body.
Warmup (5–10 min)
· Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
· Shoulder shrugs: 15 reps
· Cat-cow stretch: 10 reps
· Arm swings (backward): 10 each direction
Main Workout
1. Rowing with Bottles (Dumbbell Rows)
· Stand with feet hip-width apart, dumbbells in each hand at sides
· Hinge slightly at hips (chest up)
· Pull elbows back, bringing dumbbells to ribcage
· Squeeze shoulder blades together at the top
· Lower slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

2. Superman Holds (Back Extension)
· Lie face down, arms extended overhead (or at sides)
· Engage core, lift chest and legs off ground slightly
· Squeeze glutes and back muscles
· Hold for 15–20 seconds
· Lower slowly
· Rest 15 seconds
· Repeat: 3 sets

3. Bicep Curls with Bottles (Dumbbells)
· Stand with feet hip-width apart, dumbbells at sides (palms facing forward)
· Bend elbows, curling dumbbells up to shoulders
· Squeeze biceps at the top
· Lower slowly
· Repeat: 3 sets × 12–15 reps

4. Tricep Dips (Bedside or Chair)
· Sit on edge of sturdy chair or bed
· Hands grip edge beside hips, fingers pointing forward
· Walk feet forward, lower hips below chair level (bend elbows to 90°)
· Push back up (don't lock elbows)
· Repeat: 3 sets × 10–12 reps

5. Plank Shoulder Taps Core
· Start in plank position (hands on ground shoulder-width apart)
· Tap right hand to left shoulder (keep hips level)
· Return right hand to ground
· Tap left hand to right shoulder
· That's 1 rep (both shoulders)
· Repeat: 3 sets × 8–10 reps total

6. Dead Bug Core
· Lie on back, arms extended straight up toward ceiling
· Knees bent at 90°, shins parallel to ground
· Slowly lower right arm overhead while extending left leg straight
· Return to starting position
· Alternate sides (right arm + left leg, then left arm + right leg)
· Repeat: 3 sets × 10–12 reps total (5–6 each side)
Cool-down (5–10 min)
· Back stretch (child's pose or spinal twist): 30 sec each side
· Chest stretch: 30 sec
· Biceps/shoulder stretch: 30 sec each arm
· Neck rolls: 10 slow, gentle rolls
